BEFORE YOU BOOK

Three details worth getting right.

01 · ADVANCE BOOKING

Count days, not months.

The standard advance reservation calculation subtracts 60 calendar days from the train’s starting date. Month lengths vary, so “two months before” can give a different result. Our guide explains the calculation and exceptions.

Read the 60-day guide ↗
02 · TATKAL

Match the time to your class.

The usual Tatkal window opens one day before the train starts at its originating station: 10 AM IST for AC and 11 AM IST for non-AC. Check the train and quota on IRCTC before relying on a calculation.

Read the Tatkal guide ↗
03 · BOARDING DAY

Your train may start a day earlier.

If you board after midnight, the train may have departed its first station on the previous date. Select the correct timetable day in the calculator so the booking date is based on the train’s origin.

FROM A DATE TO A PLAN

How to use RailDate

Keep your train’s timetable nearby. The calculator handles date arithmetic; you supply the journey date and boarding day.

  1. Enter the date you will board. Choose the day of the train’s journey from its official timetable.
  2. Select advance booking or Tatkal. For Tatkal, choose AC or non-AC, then check the displayed origin date and opening time.
  3. Save the result. Copy the date or import the downloaded reminder into your calendar. Complete booking on the official IRCTC website.

What the result tells you

An opening date helps you plan. It does not confirm available seats, reserve a ticket or guarantee a booking. Special trains and quotas can have different reservation periods. Check the applicable rules, availability and booking cut-offs directly on IRCTC.

Our advance booking guide and Tatkal guide include official sources and worked examples. The website does not fetch live train schedules or seat availability.
